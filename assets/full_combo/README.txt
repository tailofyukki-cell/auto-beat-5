Full combo special media

Put PNG/JPG/WEBP/GIF images in this folder to show them on the result screen when the player gets FULL COMBO.

If you put multiple image files here, AutoBeat 5 cycles them like a short animation. Use numbered names such as:

  full_combo_001.png
  full_combo_002.png
  full_combo_003.png

Current runtime support is image-based. For video-like effects, export the video as a short PNG sequence and place the frames here.
